A clear workflow

From request to result.

GoodDeepseek works around DeepSeek and keeps the important choices visible to you.

01 / CONTEXTChoose what matters

Memory, skills, or selected workspace files.

02 / CREATEWork with DeepSeek

Draft, organize, summarize, or plan.

03 / REVIEWInspect the result

Preview text, files, and proposed changes.

04 / KEEPUse your output

Download or apply approved changes.

Your context stays under your control.

Memory and settings are stored in your browser profile. When a feature uses context, relevant information is sent to DeepSeek with your message so the model can use it. GoodDeepseek is an unofficial community project.
